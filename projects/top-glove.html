IB ASSESSMENT · 5 HOURS · KLS: 7113

Top Glove: 3-Statement Model & Comps

Completed in a timed 5-hour investment banking assessment: built the three financial statements from raw data, a DCF and a trading comparables analysis for the world's largest glove maker.

Result: comps imply an enterprise value of MYR 6.3bn, more than double the DCF value of MYR 2.9bn. The gap comes mainly from the 15% WACC in the DCF vs the 9.95x peer median multiple.

What I did

Approach and scope

  • Built the income statement, balance sheet and cash flow statement from Capital IQ data (FY2024 to FY2029 projections).
  • Projected free cash flow to firm for five years and valued the business with a Gordon growth terminal value.
  • Selected listed glove peers (Hartalega, Kossan, Riverstone, Supermax) and applied the median EV/EBITDA multiple.
  • Bridged enterprise value to equity value and reconciled the DCF and market approaches.
