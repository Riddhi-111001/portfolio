Tencent: Valuation & Monte Carlo

An FCFF valuation of Tencent Holdings prepared as a pitch for a Managing Director, then stress-tested three ways: sensitivity, scenarios and a 10,000-run Monte Carlo simulation. Group project, NUS Financial Modelling.

Result: every one of 10,000 simulations valued the stock above the current price of CNY 578.90, with a mean of CNY 677.29 (about 17% upside).

What I did

Approach and scope

  • Built an FCFF model and identified three key drivers: value-added services growth (B1), selling cost ratio (B2) and sales-to-PPE (B3).
  • Ranked the drivers with a tornado chart: revenue growth (B1) moves the price by about CNY 49, far more than the other two.
  • Built best, base and worst scenarios that combine all three drivers.
  • Ran 10,000 simulations with NORM.INV(RAND()) inputs to get a price distribution and upside/downside probabilities.
