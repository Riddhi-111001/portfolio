Bharti Airtel: DCF Valuation

A ten-year DCF of Bharti Airtel, India's second-largest telecom operator, with a full WACC derivation and three scenarios covering tariffs, 5G capex and Africa growth.

Result: base case value of Rs 1,291 per share vs a market price of Rs 1,834, about 30% below the market. Only the bull case (Rs 2,459) supports today's price.

What I did

Approach and scope

  • Derived cost of equity with CAPM using the India 10-year G-Sec yield, Damodaran's equity risk premium and a 0.5% blended country risk premium for Africa (28% of revenue).
  • Re-levered beta with the Hamada equation and used market-value weights (83.6% equity, 16.4% debt) for a 12.0% WACC.
  • Forecast revenue in three phases (FY26 to FY35), linked to ARPU, subscriber and Africa growth drivers.
  • Built bear, base and bull scenarios varying WACC, terminal growth, EBITDA margin and capex intensity.
