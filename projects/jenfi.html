Automated Transaction Tagging

At Jenfi, a revenue-based lender, underwriters tagged every bank transaction of an applicant by hand, which was slow and subjective. I turned their judgement into a rules-based Python pipeline.

Result: tagging that used to be manual now runs as a script, cutting manual review time by ~95% per 100-company batch, and system-tag coverage went from about 40% to 100%.

What I did

Approach and scope

  • Worked with the credit team to convert underwriters' subjective calls into explicit rules for each scenario and category.
  • Wrote a prioritised rules engine (a 20-level rule chain) that cleans transaction descriptions before classifying them, backed by a 160+ entity lender database compiled from primary research.
  • Benchmarked the pipeline against a 6,500+ loan book across 5+ industry verticals, reaching ~98% adjusted accuracy.
  • Extended system-tag coverage from about 40% to 100% so every transaction gets a tag.
  • Classified transactions into true customer revenue, buyer and supplier concentration, loans taken and repaid, and shareholder or related-party flows, the inputs for judging whether a business can repay.
