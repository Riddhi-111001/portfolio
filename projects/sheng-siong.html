EQUITY RESEARCH · SGX: OV8 · SEPTEMBER 2025

Sheng Siong: Buy-Side Report

A buy-side equity research report on Sheng Siong, one of Singapore's largest supermarket operators with 77 stores and about 20% market share. Group project with four classmates.

Recommendation: Stay Away. Target price S$2.12 vs S$2.09 market price, only 3% upside. Strong margins are already priced in at a ~20% premium to peers.

What I did

Approach and scope

  • Benchmarked against SEA peers (DFI Retail, Thai Beverage) and global players (Costco, Walmart) on EV/Revenue, EV/EBITDA, P/E, P/B and dividend yield.
  • Used P/E multiples as the main model and a DCF (1.5% revenue growth, 6.19% WACC, 0.75% perpetual growth) as the second, weighted equally.
  • Ran DCF sensitivity tables on WACC vs growth rate and WACC vs exit multiple.
  • Analysed industry structure, competition from FairPrice, the China expansion and governance (founding-family board).
